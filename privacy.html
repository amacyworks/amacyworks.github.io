This policy explains what data Amacy Works ("we", "us") collects through our Google Workspace add-ons, Shopify apps and this website, and how we use it. Amacy Works is the trade name of an independent developer based in Japan.

How the data is used

Document content is used only to perform the action you request, at the moment you request it, inside Google's Apps Script environment. We (the developer) cannot see your documents. We do not copy, store, log or send document content to our own servers or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • In your document (Google's document property storage and invisible named ranges): add-on settings for that document, such as the title of the Notes section, the numbering format, or which style a table uses so it can be re-applied. This stays inside the document and is removed if you delete the document.
  • In your Google account's add-on storage (Google's per-user property storage): your preferences, such as the last or default table style and any table styles you save. Only the add-on running under your account can read it.
  • Only if you buy a paid plan: your email address and the status of your subscription (see section 3). When paid plans are available, the add-on sends your Google account email address to our license server to check whether you have a paid plan; nothing else is sent.

Limited Use

Amacy Works' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data, including Google Workspace API data, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI/ML models. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it, except with your explicit permission (for example when you send us a document for support), for security purposes, or to comply with the law.

1a. How we protect your data

  • Processing stays at Google. Document content is processed only by Google Apps Script on Google's infrastructure and is never transferred to systems we operate.
  • Encryption in transit. All communication between your browser, Google, our license server and our payment partner uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Encryption at rest. The only personal data we hold (email address and subscription status for paid plans) is stored with Cloudflare, which encrypts it at rest. Payment details are held by our payment partner, which is PCI DSS certified; we never receive or store card numbers.
  • Access control. Only the developer can access the license records and the add-on projects, through accounts protected by two-step verification. The add-ons authenticate to the license server with a secret key that is kept in Google's server-side script storage and is never shown to users.
  • Data minimization. The add-ons request only the "current document" permission and keep no copies of document content, so there is no document data on our side to leak.
  • Deletion and incidents. You can uninstall an add-on at any time and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request we delete your license record (subject to tax-record rules). If we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected users and authorities as required by law.

2. Shopify apps

When a merchant installs one of our Shopify apps, we receive the shop's domain, the access token Shopify issues, and only the store data the app needs to work (listed on each app's page). We do not access customer personal data unless an app's feature requires it, and we state it on that app's page when it does. We respond to Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks: we delete shop data within 48 hours of uninstallation (shop redact) and handle customer data requests and erasure requests as required.

3. Payments

Workspace add-on payments are processed by our payment partner, which acts as the merchant of record. We never see or store your full card number. Shopify app charges are billed by Shopify.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run our services (Google, Shopify, our payment partner, and our hosting provider Cloudflare), or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep license records while your subscription is active and for as long as tax law requires afterwards. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing support@amacyworks.com.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA, the UK and California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to processing. Email us to exercise these rights; we will respond within 30 days.

7. International transfers

We operate from Japan, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Japan has an adequacy decision from the European Commission.
